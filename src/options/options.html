Edge → Raindrop Sync

Sync Edge bookmarks with Raindrop.io, mirroring your folder shape. Choose how traffic flows; one-way mode also asks what to do with the Edge copy after upload.

Settings

Uploads Edge → Raindrop only. Below, choose whether Edge keeps a local copy after a confirmed upload, or offloads (deletes locally).

Keeps bookmarks in both Edge and Raindrop: pulls Raindrop → Edge under your root, and propagates user deletes both ways. Raindrop-only metadata (tags, notes, highlights) is never overwritten from Edge.

Deleting a bookmark in Edge or Raindrop removes the paired item in the other system. To offload a specific folder (upload then remove from Edge only), set that folder’s policy to Offload (delete from Edge) below — that cleanup does not delete Raindrop.

  • Delete from Edge — After a confirmed upload, remove the Edge bookmark. Raindrop keeps the copy. This is the default for one-way offload.
  • Keep in Edge — Leave the Edge bookmark after upload (mirror, don’t clear local).
  • Exclude — Do not sync by default; use folder policies to opt folders in.

Global default is keep in both places. Use folder policies for exceptions: Exclude a subtree, or Offload (delete from Edge) for folders you still want cleared locally after upload.
