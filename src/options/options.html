Edge → Raindrop Sync

Sync Edge bookmarks with Raindrop.io, mirroring your folder shape. Choose one-way upload or bidirectional sync, and per-folder whether items are deleted locally, kept, or skipped.

Settings

Bidirectional mode propagates user deletes both ways: deleting a bookmark in Edge or Raindrop removes the paired item in the other system. Raindrop-only metadata (tags, notes, highlights) is never overwritten from Edge. Policy “Sync & delete” still removes the Edge copy after upload without deleting Raindrop.
